We believe in authentic integration. Instead of reading from textbooks, we will utilize active developer workflows. Students will use industry tools like integrated development environments (IDEs), cloud command lines, and professional version control platforms like GitHub.

Our curriculum is structured to align precisely with official educational guidelines ensuring students build digital citizenship alongside raw technical skill.
